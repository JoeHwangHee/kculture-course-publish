# Notice on Restrictions for Tourist Visits to Bukchon Special Management Area

발행처: Seoul Metropolitan Government (English)

## 북촌한옥마을

영문 공지: 레드존 제한, 과태료, 일요일은 골목길 쉬는날 (2024-11-15 게시)

원문 인용: A fine of KRW 100,000 will be imposed for any violations within the restricted hours. Sunday is a “rest day” for the alleyways
